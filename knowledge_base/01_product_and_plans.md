# AcmeFlow product and plan guide

## Product overview

AcmeFlow is a fictional cloud project-management product for small software teams. It provides workspaces, projects, task boards, comments, attachments, automations, and CSV exports. This knowledge base is the authoritative source for the exam prototype; the product does not represent a real company.

## Starter plan

The Starter plan is free. A Starter workspace supports a maximum of 3 active members, 5 active projects, and 500 MB of total attachment storage. Archived projects do not count toward the 5-project limit, but their attachments still count toward storage.

Starter workspaces can use the board and list views. They cannot use custom automations, audit-log export, or priority support.

## Team plan

The Team plan costs EUR 12 per workspace per month or EUR 120 per workspace per year. It supports up to 25 active members, unlimited active projects, 20 GB of attachment storage, and 20 active automations.

The Team plan includes email support during business hours. It does not include audit-log export or a contractual response-time agreement.

## Business plan

The Business plan costs EUR 24 per active member per month and is billed monthly. It supports unlimited members, unlimited projects, 200 GB of attachment storage, unlimited automations, audit-log export, and priority support.

Business subscriptions require at least 5 active paid members. Deactivated members stop counting at the beginning of the next billing cycle.

## Member and guest rules

Every person with access to more than one project counts as an active member. A guest can access exactly one project and does not count toward the member limit. Each workspace can have up to 10 guests on Starter, 50 guests on Team, and 200 guests on Business.

If a Starter workspace already has 3 active members, a fourth member cannot accept an invitation until an existing member is deactivated or the workspace is upgraded.
