# AcmeFlow billing and refund policy

## Annual subscription purchases

A customer can receive a full refund for a first annual Team subscription when the request is submitted within 14 calendar days of the original purchase. The support agent must verify the workspace ID and purchase date before escalating the refund request to Billing Operations.

Support agents cannot issue refunds directly and must not promise that a refund has already been approved.

## Annual renewals

An automatically renewed annual Team subscription is eligible for a full refund when the customer contacts support within 7 calendar days of the renewal charge and the workspace has not created a new project or uploaded an attachment after renewal.

If the workspace was actively used after renewal, Billing Operations decides whether to offer a partial credit. The support agent must escalate the case and must not calculate or promise a specific credit.

## Monthly subscriptions

Monthly Team and Business payments are normally non-refundable once a billing period begins. Verified duplicate charges and charges caused by an AcmeFlow billing error are exceptions and must be escalated to Billing Operations.

Cancellation stops the next renewal. It does not immediately remove paid features; the workspace remains on its paid plan until the end of the current billing period.

## Cancellation procedure

Workspace owners can cancel through **Workspace settings → Billing → Manage subscription → Cancel renewal**. Administrators and ordinary members cannot cancel a subscription.

If the owner cannot access the workspace, support must first follow the account-recovery procedure. Support must never cancel a subscription based only on an unverified email request.

## Billing escalation information

A billing escalation should include the workspace ID, charge date, amount, currency, last four characters of the invoice ID, and whether the workspace was used after the charge. Never request a full payment-card number or bank-account credentials.
